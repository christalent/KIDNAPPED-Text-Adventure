KIDNAPPED (?)

Inspired by an entry on the discussion board, I decided to have a
go at this game. It's quite probably the easiest adventure game
I've ever played. Hmm… of course, King's Ransom is even shorter,
but this certainly comes in a close second.
The plot is non-existant but revolves around a simple setup which
has you locked on the 9th floor of a building. You have to make
your way down to the ground floor. All floors have to be solved
seperately.

Floor 9: N, GET CHAIR, READ NOTE (watch tv!), N, GET BROOM, S, S,
S, GET KEY (with the broom), N, W, S, READ GRAFFITI (a warning),
N, N, WATCH TV (the power will be cut), S, E, E, S, OPEN CABINET
(with the key), GET FLASHLIGHT, GET TAPE, LIGHT FLASHLIGHT, N, N,
DROP CHAIR, CLIMB CHAIR (you reach the trap door), TAPE WIRES, D,
PRESS BUTTON

Floor 8: S, GET PILL, N, W, W, GET ROPE, E, DROP PILL (into the
aquarium), GET PIRANHA, E, S, DROP PIRANHA (it devours the poor
dog!!), E, TIE ROPE, CLIMB ROPE

Floor 7: W, GET GLUE, GET STEP, E, S, E, E, GET SHEET (of
copper), W, W, N, N, MAKE KEY, GET KEY, S, E, OPEN DOOR (with the
key), GO DOOR, GLUE STEP (the staircase is repaired), CLIMB
STAIRS

Floor 6: N, N, GET GAS (helium), S, S, E, GET BALLOON, W, S, GET
GUN, N, W, W, CLIMB LEDGE, W, CLIMB WINDOW, GET DOLLAR (you stick
up the kidnapper with the gun), W, E, CLIMB WINDOW, E, N, READ
SIGN, PUT DOLLAR (you buy some string), GET STRING, INFLATE
BALLOON, TIE BALLOON, S, W, CLIMB LEDGE, JUMP

Floor 5: W, S, GET NEEDLES, N, N, GET YARN, S, E, E (there's a
fire to the north), S, PRESS BUTTON (sprinklers are turned on and
douse the fire – you remove your wet clothes), KNIT CLOTHES (you
quickly produce a new set), N, N, GET KEY, S, W, OPEN DOOR, GO
DOOR, CLIMB STAIRS

Floor 4: N, PRESS BUTTON (this fills the pool with water), S, S,
GET BOOK, READ BOOK (you now know how to swim), N, E, SWIM, N,
GET UMBRELLA, OPEN UMBRELLA, JUMP

Floor 3: N, READ GRAFFITI (a hint), S, E, E, N, GET FLUID, GET
SOLUTION, S, DRINK FLUID (you turn into the strong Mr. Hyde),
OPEN DOOR, GO DOOR, DRINK SOLUTION (you revert to normal), CLIMB
SLIDE

Floor 2: (you are faced by a huge alligator) GET CUP, GET WATER,
W, POUR WATER (the plant grows to reach the ceiling), CLIMB
PLANT, E, GET FLUTE (you see a rope below), PLAY FLUTE (the magic
rope reaches up to you), CLIMB ROPE, CLIMB STAIRS

Floor 1: GET ROPE, GET KEY (well, pianos have keys!), E, GET BOOK
("How to walk a tightrope), READ BOOK, TIE ROPE (to the stake),
THROW ROPE (it catches on to the hook), CLIMB ROPE (you cross the
pit), OPEN DOOR, GO DOOR (you have escaped!)

Jacob Gunness – September 2004
