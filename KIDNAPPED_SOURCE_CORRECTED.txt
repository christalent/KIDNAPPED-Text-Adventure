KIDNAPPED! -- SOURCE-CORRECTED TRANSCRIPTION (SoftSide Vol.3 No.03, Dec 1980)
Corrected 2026-11-04 from native 300 ppi renders of KIDNAPPED_Original.pdf,
cross-checked against two independent OCR passes of the same listing:
  * the PDF text layer (KIDNAPPED_OCR.txt)
  * Archive.org issue 27 djvu text (SoftSide Dec 1980 magazine scan)
  * Archive.org issue 34 djvu text (SoftSide Jul 1981 Apple/Atari re-listing)

WHY THE OCR IS WRONG
  The printed listing is degraded and BOTH OCR passes systematically read the digit
  4 as 1 (see FLOOR9_GROUND_TRUTH.md: listing line 23 OCR 'W=11' vs pixels 'W=14').
  Every room number below is therefore given in its PRINTED value.

CONFIDENCE LEGEND
  [PIX]  read directly from native 300 ppi pixels
  [OCR2] both independent OCR passes agree after the 4->1 repair
  [XCHK] additionally confirmed by an audited floor module in this repo
  [UNRES] unresolved -- needs a fresh pixel read before use
